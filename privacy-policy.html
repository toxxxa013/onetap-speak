Privacy Policy for OneTap Speak

Effective date: June 17, 2026

OneTap Speak is a simple voice-to-voice translation app for travel, family, and everyday communication.

This Privacy Policy explains how OneTap Speak handles information when you use the app.

Summary

OneTap Speak is designed to work without an account and without a developer-operated backend server.

We do not operate our own servers for storing your voice, conversations, or translations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your conversations for advertising.

Information the app uses

OneTap Speak may use the following information on your device:

  • microphone audio, only when you press and hold a speech button;
  • recognized speech text;
  • translated text;
  • selected language pair;
  • selected app language;
  • selected voice and playback settings;
  • free trial usage count;
  • purchase entitlement status provided by Apple.

Microphone and speech recognition

OneTap Speak uses microphone access only to capture the phrase you choose to translate.

The app uses Apple system technologies for speech recognition, translation, and speech playback. OneTap Speak does not run its own cloud speech recognition or translation server.

The app does not intentionally store your audio recordings after processing.

Apple system services may be subject to Apple's own privacy practices and settings. You can learn more at apple.com/privacy.

Translation

OneTap Speak uses Apple system translation features and local correction rules inside the app to improve the final displayed translation.

OneTap Speak does not send your translations to a developer-owned server.

Purchases

OneTap Speak may offer an in-app purchase to unlock the full version.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. OneTap Speak does not receive or store your payment card information.

Refunds, billing, purchase history, and Family Sharing are handled by Apple according to Apple's App Store rules.

Free trial usage

OneTap Speak may provide a limited number of successful translations before purchase.

The app may store a local trial usage counter on the device, including in the device Keychain, to remember how many successful free translations have been used.

Recognition errors, empty recordings, permission failures, and canceled attempts are not intended to count as successful translations.

Data we do not collect

OneTap Speak does not require you to create an account.

OneTap Speak does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or address book.

OneTap Speak does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell personal data.

Legal, medical, emergency, and professional use

OneTap Speak is intended for everyday communication, travel, and family use.

It is not a medical, legal, police, immigration, financial, emergency, or professional interpretation service.

Translations may be inaccurate or incomplete. Do not rely on the app as the only source of information in high-risk or professional situations.

Children's privacy

OneTap Speak is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same URL with a new effective date.
